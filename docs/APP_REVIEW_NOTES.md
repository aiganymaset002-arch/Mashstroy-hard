# App Review Notes (вставить в App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes)

```
MASHSTROY AI Control is an industrial monitoring and control app for
MASHSTROY conveyor equipment used by trained factory staff.

The physical conveyor cannot be connected during review, so the app includes
a built-in simulator (demo mode) that behaves like the ESP32 controller and
its sensors. All screens work in demo mode.

How to review:
1. Launch the app and accept the safety rules.
2. Sign in with the demo account below (or any e-mail and a 6+ character
   password), keep role "Engineer" (full access), accept the Terms.
3. Tabs: Обзор (dashboard), Управление (control: Start/Stop/E-Stop, modes),
   ИИ (AI diagnostics), Аварии (fault center), Ещё (sensors, camera, charts).
4. To see AI diagnostics and faults: Ещё → Симуляция неисправностей → turn on
   "Износ подшипника #2" and wait ~30 seconds.
5. Account deletion: Ещё → Удалить аккаунт.

Demo account: review@mashstroy.kz / Review2026

The app does not replace the physical emergency stop; this is stated on
first launch and in the Terms. No tracking, ads or third-party analytics.
UI language: Russian.
```
